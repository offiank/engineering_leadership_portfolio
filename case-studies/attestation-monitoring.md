# Attestation & Monitoring Service

**Role:** Engineering Manager (Senior Development Lead) · Girmiti Software · 2021–2026
**Domain:** Payments / Fraud Prevention

## Context

Girmiti's payments business needed a way to verify the integrity of mPOS (mobile point-of-sale) devices in the field and detect tampering or compromise before it could be used for fraud. No such capability existed — this was a greenfield build.

## What I Did

- Designed and led the Attestation & Monitoring service from scratch, defining architecture, scope, and delivery plan.
- Hired and built a 7–8 person Java/React engineering team specifically for this product.
- Directed the design of cryptographic device-attestation flows deployed across tens of thousands of mPOS devices in the field.
- Owned the roadmap and cross-functional coordination with security, fraud, and device-operations stakeholders.

## Outcome

- Blocked **10,000+ fraudulent transactions** and flagged compromised devices in production.
- Product was recognized company-wide for its security impact and delivery quality.
- Became a reference architecture for subsequent fraud-prevention initiatives within the org.
