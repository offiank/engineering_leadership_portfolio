**Role:** Engineering Manager (Senior Development Lead) · Girmiti Software · 2021–2026
**Domain:** Payments / Banking Infrastructure

- Balanced ongoing feature delivery against the operational rigor required for a national payments system.

- Delivered a stable integration layer used across all 12 partner banks in production.
