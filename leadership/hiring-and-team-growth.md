# Hiring & Team Growth

**Context:** Senior Development Lead (Engineering Manager), Girmiti Software, 03/2021–02/2026

## Scope

Owned hiring, roadmap planning, OKRs, and delivery across four engineering teams spanning payments, IoT, and SaaS — a 15-engineer organization.

## What I Did

- Hired 7–8 Java/React engineers from scratch to stand up a new fraud-prevention team.
- Built onboarding and ramp practices that cut new-hire ramp time from **6 weeks to 3**.
- Coached and developed individual contributors, promoting **3 ICs into lead roles**.
- Ran performance management and succession planning across the org.
- Grew and retained a 15-person organization at **95%+ retention over four years**.

## Why It Matters

Retention and internal promotion at this rate, sustained over four years across multiple teams, reflects a deliberate investment in coaching, career growth paths, and team health — not just delivery output.
