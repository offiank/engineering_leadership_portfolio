# Engineering Metrics & AI-Assisted Delivery Rollout

**Context:** Senior Development Lead (Engineering Manager), Girmiti Software, 03/2021–02/2026

## What I Did

- Established DORA and SPACE metric baselines across the engineering organization to make delivery performance measurable rather than anecdotal.
- Rolled out GitHub Copilot and Gemini to **40+ engineers** as part of an AI-assisted development initiative.
- Tracked adoption and impact against the established baselines.

## Outcome

- Raised delivery velocity by **20%**.
- Cut integration defects by **25%**.

## Why It Matters

Tooling rollouts only create value when paired with measurement. Tying the Copilot/Gemini rollout to existing DORA/SPACE baselines made the impact demonstrable rather than assumed.
